TOOBUR — Bruteforce / probe 0x0AF1

Chrome + Web Bluetooth. Serve over HTTP: cd htmlapp && python3 -m http.server 8000 → http://localhost:8000/toobur-bruteforce-0af1.html

  • 0x0AF1 = legacy ID115-style health TX (write). 0x0AF2 = matching notify RX.
  • This tool writes payloads to 0x0AF1 only and records notifications on 0x0AF2 within a timeout. Optional: also listen on 0x0AF7 to spot cross-channel replies.
  • Stop the scan if the watch misbehaves; keep delay reasonable (≥50 ms).
